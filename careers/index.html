Careers

Help build fast, free, privacy-first tools used by people all over the world — from wherever you work best.

How we work

  • Fully remote: every role is virtual. Work from anywhere with a good connection.
  • Async first: clear written briefs over long meetings; ship, then talk.
  • Privacy by design: we build tools that never upload user data, and we hold ourselves to that standard.
  • Small, real impact: what you build goes live to real users quickly.

Roles we hire for

  • Internships: for students and early-career makers in engineering, design, content and marketing.
  • Full-time: for experienced people who want to own a product area end to end.
