How It Works

How to Play Minesweeper in 3 Steps

1

Make your opening click

Your very first click is guaranteed never to be a mine — mines are laid after you click, leaving a safe 3×3 opening around your square.

2

Read the numbers & flag mines

Each number tells you how many mines touch that square’s 8 neighbours. Right-click (or switch to Flag mode on touch, or press F) to mark suspected mines.

3

Chord to clear faster

Once a numbered square has the right number of flags around it, click the number itself to reveal all its remaining neighbours at once. Reveal every non-mine square to win.

FAQ

Minesweeper — Frequently Asked Questions

Can I lose on the first click?

Never. Mines are placed only after your first click, and the clicked square plus its 8 immediate neighbours are kept mine-free so you always get a playable opening.

What does Undo do in Minesweeper?

If you accidentally click a mine, pressing Undo (Z) steps back one move so you can finish a long Expert board without starting over.

How do I flag on a phone or tablet?

Tap the Mode: Dig / Flag button below the board to switch your tap action between digging and flagging.

How does the 16×30 Expert board fit on a phone?

On narrow screens the board wrapper scrolls horizontally inside the card so every cell stays large enough to tap accurately without zooming the page.

Is anything uploaded or tracked while I play?

No. The game runs entirely in your browser tab. Your best score or win tally is saved only in your browser’s localStorage and cleared when you press Reset.
