How It Works

How to Play Connect Four in 3 Steps

1

Choose 1P or 2 Players

Play against the built-in minimax computer (Easy, Medium or Hard) or switch to 2 Players to share the screen.

2

Drop a disc

Click any of the 7 columns — or press keys 1–7 — to drop your disc into the lowest open slot of that column.

3

Connect four in any direction

The first player to line up four discs horizontally, vertically or diagonally wins; the winning four are highlighted.

FAQ

Connect Four — Frequently Asked Questions

How strong is the Hard computer?

Hard searches 7 plies ahead using alpha–beta pruning with centre-column move ordering and threat evaluation. It spots forced four-in-a-row traps several moves early while still replying in a few milliseconds.

Why is the centre column so important?

Any horizontal or diagonal four-in-a-row spanning the board has to pass through the middle column (column 4), so controlling it gives you the most winning lines.

What does Undo take back in 1P mode?

Both the computer’s reply and your last disc, returning the board to your previous turn.

How do I clear the win/draw tally?

Press Reset in the toolbar (or Esc) to wipe stored scores and restore default options.

Is anything uploaded or tracked while I play?

No. The game runs entirely in your browser tab. Your best score or win tally is saved only in your browser’s localStorage and cleared when you press Reset.
