How It Works

How to Play Sudoku in 3 Steps

1

Pick a difficulty

Easy removes ~36 numbers, Medium ~46 and Hard ~52. Every puzzle is generated on the spot and verified to have a single solution.

2

Fill & take notes

Click a cell (or move with the arrow keys) and press 1–9. Toggle Notes (N) to jot candidate digits inside a cell; placing a number automatically clears that digit’s notes in its row, column and 3×3 box.

3

Complete all 81 cells

Duplicate digits in any row, column or box glow red so you can spot mistakes early. Fill every cell without conflicts to solve the puzzle.

FAQ

Sudoku — Frequently Asked Questions

Does every puzzle have a unique solution?

Yes. The generator builds a complete valid grid and only removes a clue if the solver confirms there is still exactly one way to finish the board.

Are there any “guess” moves required?

Easy and Medium puzzles are solvable with standard single-candidate and hidden-single logic. Hard puzzles may require pairs or box-line reductions, never blind guessing.

What does highlighting show?

Selecting a cell softly tints its row, column and 3×3 box, and highlights every other cell holding the same digit so you can scan across boxes quickly.

Can I play without conflict highlighting?

Open Game options and untick “Highlight conflicts” if you want a pure paper-style solve; the board is still checked when the last cell is filled.

Is anything uploaded or tracked while I play?

No. The game runs entirely in your browser tab. Your best score or win tally is saved only in your browser’s localStorage and cleared when you press Reset.
