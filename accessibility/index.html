Accessibility Statement

Useful tools should be usable by everyone. Here is where we stand and how to tell us when we fall short.

Our goal

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA across the site.

What we have in place

  • Keyboard access to navigation, search and tool controls, with visible focus outlines.
  • Labelled form controls and descriptive buttons for screen readers.
  • Light and dark themes that follow your system setting, with contrast checked in both.
  • Respect for prefers-reduced-motion — animations calm down when you ask your system for less motion.
  • Touch targets of at least 44 px on touch screens, and layouts that reflow from phone to widescreen.
  • Native HTML elements (buttons, details, dialogs) wherever possible, so assistive technology works as expected.

Known limitations

  • Some games (for example Pong, Tetris and Flappy Bird) rely on fast visual feedback and may be hard to play with a screen reader.
  • Visual tools such as image editors, colour pickers and previews depend on sight for parts of their output; we provide text values (sizes, hex codes) wherever we can.
  • A few tools load third-party libraries whose interfaces we don't fully control.

Report a barrier

If something stops you from using Toolity, email hello@toolity.in with the page, your browser and any assistive technology you use. We read every report and prioritise fixes.
