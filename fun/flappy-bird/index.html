How It Works

How to Play Flappy Bird in 3 Steps

1

Flap to start

Press Space or ↑, click, or tap the board. Every flap gives the bird a small upward push; gravity does the rest.

2

Thread the pipes

Each pair of pipes has a gap — fly through it to score a point. Easy has wide gaps and slow pipes; Hard is tight and quick.

3

Beat your best

Touching a pipe, the ground or the ceiling ends the run. Your best score per difficulty is stored on this device only.

FAQ

Flappy Bird — Frequently Asked Questions

How do I flap?

Press Space or ↑, click the board with a mouse, or tap it on a touchscreen. Short, regular taps are easier to control than long gaps between flaps.

Why does the bird feel heavy?

Gravity pulls constantly while each flap only sets a fixed upward speed, exactly like the original. Flap a little earlier than feels natural and aim for the lower half of each gap.

What changes between difficulties?

Easy widens the gap and slows the pipes; Hard narrows the gap and speeds them up. Pipe heights are random on all three.

Where is my best score saved?

Only in your browser's localStorage under the key toolity_flappy, separately per difficulty. Nothing is sent anywhere; Reset or clearing site data removes it.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes — tap anywhere on the board to flap. The board is portrait, scales to your screen, and the game pauses by itself when you switch apps.
