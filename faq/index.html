Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about privacy, pricing and how Toolity tools work.

General

Is Toolity really free?

Yes. Every tool is free to use, with no sign-up, no trial and no watermarks.

Do I need an account?

No. Open a tool and use it — there are no accounts or logins.

Can I use Toolity for commercial work?

Yes. You keep full ownership of everything you create; see our Terms of Service.

How do I request a new tool or report a bug?

Email hello@toolity.in or use the topic links on our Contact page.

Privacy & data

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Tools process files inside your browser on your own device. Your files and text are never sent to Toolity.

Do any tools use the internet?

A few Labs tools that read other websites (Open Graph Preview, Meta Tag Extractor, Redirect Checker, Favicon Extractor) may send only the URL you type to a public relay. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

Does Toolity use cookies?

We use anonymous Google Analytics cookies to understand which tools are popular, and your browser's local storage to remember preferences like dark mode. See the Cookie Policy.

Using the tools

Is there a file size limit?

There is no fixed limit — it depends on your device's memory. Very large videos or PDFs work best on a desktop browser.

Does it work offline?

Once a tool page has loaded, processing happens on your device. Some tools download a library (for example, a video encoder) the first time you use them, so stay online for the first run.

Which browsers are supported?

Current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari on desktop and mobile.

Does Toolity work on my phone?

Yes. Every tool is designed for mobile, tablet and desktop.

Can AI agents use Toolity?

Yes. In browsers that support WebMCP, AI agents can operate every tool directly, still entirely in your browser. A plain-text index is at /llms.txt.
