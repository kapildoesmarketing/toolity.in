How It Works

How to Use Text to Handwriting in 3 Steps

1

Write or paste

Type your text, or drop a .txt / .md file. Blank lines make paragraphs; a line containing only --- forces a page break. Add an optional header line (name, roll number, date) for page 1.

2

Pick the hand, paper and ink

Choose one of 12 handwriting styles — or upload your own .ttf/.otf — set the ink colour, paper type and size. Raise Pen pressure & jitter for natural variation: every letter gets a tiny rotation, baseline wobble and ink-density change, so no two lines look identical. Settings hold line height, spacing, margins and the scanner effect.

3

Download

Pages paginate automatically; flick through them above the preview. Download the current page as PNG, every page as a ZIP of PNGs, or everything as one PDF sized exactly A4 / Letter / A5 — ready to print or submit.

FAQ

Text to Handwriting — Frequently Asked Questions

How do I make it look less like a font?

Three things matter: jitter (40–70 % is the sweet spot), a hand that is a little irregular (Homemade Apple, Nothing You Could Do, Zeyada), and the scanner effect, which adds paper grain, a soft page shadow and a slight skew like a phone photo of a notebook. Blue ink on ruled paper with the text sitting on the lines completes it.

Can I use my own handwriting?

Yes — upload a .ttf or .otf font of your handwriting (free services like Calligraphr turn a scanned sample into a font). The file is loaded with the browser’s FontFace API and never leaves your device. It is not saved between visits.

Why are some characters boxes or in a different style?

Decorative handwriting fonts often cover only Latin letters. Characters outside the font (Devanagari, CJK, some symbols) fall back to a system font. Use the upload option with a font that covers your script.

What resolution are the downloads?

Pages render at 200 dpi (A4 = 1654 × 2339 px), which prints crisply. The PDF embeds those images at true A4 / Letter / A5 size using jsPDF, loaded only when you download a PDF.

Is there a length limit?

No fixed limit — a 50-page essay works, but each page is rendered as an image, so very long documents take a few seconds to export and the ZIP/PDF grows by roughly 300–500 KB per page.

Is my input sent anywhere?

No. Everything is computed by JavaScript in your browser tab. Nothing is uploaded, logged or stored by Toolity.
