FAQ

Share & Message Link Generator — Frequently Asked Questions

Why doesn’t Facebook or LinkedIn show my message?

Both only accept a URL in share links and read the title, description and image from the page’s Open Graph tags. Check how your page will look with the Open Graph Preview.

What is the difference from the WhatsApp Link Creator?

This tool makes a WhatsApp share link — the sender picks who to send it to. The WhatsApp Link Creator opens a chat with one specific phone number.

Which SMS style should I use?

iPhones expect sms:number&body= and Android expects sms:number?body=. The universal form ?&body= works on both in current versions, so it is the default; pick a specific style only if you know the device.

Do the share buttons track clicks?

No. The HTML snippet is plain <a> links with rel="noopener noreferrer" — no JavaScript, no third-party scripts, no tracking pixels. Style them with your own CSS.

Is my input sent anywhere?

No. Everything is computed by JavaScript in your browser tab. Nothing is uploaded, logged or stored by Toolity.
