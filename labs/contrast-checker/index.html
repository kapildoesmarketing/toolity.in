FAQ

Contrast Checker — Frequently Asked Questions

What do the thresholds mean?

WCAG 2.2: normal text needs 4.5:1 for AA and 7:1 for AAA; large text (≥24px, or ≥19px bold) needs 3:1 for AA and 4.5:1 for AAA; non-text UI parts such as input borders and icons need 3:1. The ratio is computed from relative luminance exactly as the spec defines.

Does semi-transparent text work?

Yes. A text colour with alpha is composited over the background first, then measured — the same thing your screen does. Transparent backgrounds are composited over white.

How is the fix colour chosen?

The hue and saturation stay the same; lightness is moved step by step away from the background until the target ratio is met. It is the smallest change that passes, not necessarily the prettiest — treat it as a starting point.

Is passing AA enough?

AA is the legal baseline in most jurisdictions. AAA is recommended for body text people read for long stretches. Also remember contrast is only one check — colour should never be the only way information is conveyed.

Is my input sent anywhere?

No. Everything is computed by JavaScript in your browser tab. Nothing is uploaded, logged or stored by Toolity.
