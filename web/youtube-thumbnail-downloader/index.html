FAQ

YouTube Thumbnail Downloader — Frequently Asked Questions

Is downloading a YouTube thumbnail legal?

Thumbnails are publicly served by YouTube's image CDN, and this tool simply shows you that public URL. The image itself remains the copyright of the channel owner — use it for personal reference, previews or with permission, not for re-uploading as your own.

Why is HD (1080p) not available for some videos?

YouTube only generates maxresdefault.jpg when the uploader provided a high-resolution custom thumbnail. Older, auto-generated or Shorts thumbnails often stop at 480p, so the tool falls back to HQ automatically.

Which YouTube link formats are supported?

Standard watch?v= links, youtu.be short links, /shorts/ links, /embed/ links and raw 11-character video IDs all work.

Does this tool go through a Toolity server?

No. The video ID is parsed in your browser and the preview loads straight from img.youtube.com. Only the Download button may route through a public image proxy to work around browser CORS limits — Toolity never sees or stores the image.

Can I download thumbnails for private or unlisted videos?

Unlisted videos usually work because their thumbnails are still public. Private videos do not expose thumbnails on the CDN, so nothing can be fetched.
