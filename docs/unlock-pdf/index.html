FAQ

Unlock PDF — Frequently Asked Questions

Can this crack a password I do not know?

No, and nothing in a browser realistically can for modern AES encryption. It removes protection from files you can already open — a document you were sent with its password, or one you protected yourself and no longer want locked.

What are "owner restrictions"?

A PDF can open without a password yet forbid printing, copying or editing. Those flags are only enforced when the file stays encrypted, so decrypting it with the (empty) open password removes them. The tool detects this case and tells you no password is needed.

Will the content change?

No. Pages, text, images, links and form fields are written out exactly as they were, just without the encryption layer. File size may shift by a few kilobytes.

Is it legal to remove a PDF password?

For your own documents or ones you are authorised to use, yes. Bypassing protection on a file you have no right to is a different matter — please respect the owner's wishes and local law.

Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?

No. The document is parsed and rewritten by pdf-lib and rendered by PDF.js (the engine Firefox uses) entirely inside your browser. Nothing is transmitted or stored.
