FAQ

Grayscale PDF — Frequently Asked Questions

Is the text still selectable afterwards?

No. Pages are re-rendered as grayscale images, so text becomes part of the picture. If you need selectable text in grayscale, your PDF editor's print-to-PDF with a grayscale printer profile is the right tool.

Will the file get smaller?

Usually, for colour-heavy pages — a grayscale JPEG at 150 dpi is far smaller than embedded colour photos. Text-only PDFs can grow, because vector text is tiny and images are not.

How is the conversion done?

Each page is drawn by PDF.js onto a canvas, desaturated with a luminance filter (the same maths printers use) and saved as a JPEG page with pdf-lib.

Can I convert only some pages?

Yes — enter a range in Settings; other pages are copied unchanged (and stay in colour).

Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?

No. The document is read by PDF.js (the engine Firefox uses) and rewritten by pdf-lib entirely inside your browser. Nothing is transmitted or stored.
