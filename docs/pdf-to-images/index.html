    FAQ

    PDF to Images — Frequently Asked Questions

    Which resolution should I pick?

    72 dpi matches the PDF's nominal size in pixels; 150 dpi is sharp on screens and in slides; 300 dpi is what printers expect. Higher dpi means larger files and slower rendering — a 300 dpi A4 page is about 2 500 × 3 500 px.

    Why is PNG so much larger than JPG?

    PNG is lossless, so photographic pages compress poorly. Use JPG or WebP for scans and photos, PNG for text, line art and anything with transparency.

    Is there a page limit?

    No hard limit; rendering happens one page at a time with a progress bar. Several hundred pages at 300 dpi will take a while and use memory for the ZIP — split the PDF first if it is huge.

    Can I extract the original embedded images instead of rendering pages?

    Not with this tool — it renders what you see on each page. Extracting embedded image streams is on the list if people ask.

    Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?

    No. The document is read by PDF.js (the engine Firefox uses) and rewritten by pdf-lib entirely inside your browser. Nothing is transmitted or stored.
