Frequently Asked Questions

Video Speed Changer FAQ

Common questions about video speed adjustments, audio pitch, and client-side processing.

Does changing video speed upload my files to any remote server?
No. Toolity.in runs 100% locally in your browser using the HTML5 Canvas, AudioContext, and MediaRecorder APIs. Your video files never touch any external server or cloud infrastructure.
What is the difference between "Pitch Correct" and "Pitch Shift"?
Pitch Correct preserves natural human voice tone so fast speech doesn't sound squeaky (chipmunk effect) and slow speech doesn't sound unnaturally deep. Pitch Shift scales the audio frequencies proportionally with playback speed for vintage vinyl record effects.
Can I speed up just a specific section of my video?
Yes! Use the Trim Clip Boundary start and end sliders in the left pane to define the exact segment you wish to speed up or slow down before exporting.
What video formats are supported?
You can import MP4, WebM, MOV, AVI, MKV, and OGG formats. Exported videos are rendered as universally compatible MP4 (H.264) or WebM (VP9) files suitable for social media, YouTube, and Discord.
