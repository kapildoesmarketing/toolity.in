How It Works

Universal Video Transcoding in Your Browser

Convert, trim, rescale, and extract audio from any video format in 3 simple steps.

1

Upload Video File

Drop your MP4, WebM, MOV, MKV, or AVI file into the workspace. The browser reads metadata and initializes the decoding pipeline.

2

Choose Format & Resolution

Select your desired output container (MP4, WebM, MOV, or Audio), adjust resolution scaling (1080p, 720p), framerate, and clip boundaries.

3

Transcode & Download

Click Convert to render the new stream in memory. Watch the preview in the output player and download directly to your device.
