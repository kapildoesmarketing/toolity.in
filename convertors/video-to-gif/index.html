How It Works

Converting Videos to High-Quality GIFs

Turn screen recordings, video clips, and memes into animated GIFs in 3 simple steps.

1

Upload & Select Clip

Drop your MP4, WebM, or MOV video into the workspace. Use the dual-handle trimmer to define the exact start and end timestamps.

2

Configure Framerate & Size

Select your desired FPS (10–15 fps recommended) and output width (480px). Choose color quality and playback speed.

3

Convert & Download

Click Convert to compile the GIF in your browser memory with multi-worker acceleration. Copy to clipboard or download directly to your device.
