How It Works

Converting Animated GIFs to High-Definition Video

Transform short GIFs into universal MP4 and WebM video clips in 3 simple steps.

1

Upload Animated GIF

Drop your animated .gif file into the workspace or click to browse. The browser decodes all frames and timing metadata locally.

2

Configure Loops & Speed

Choose your loop multiplier (e.g. 3x to turn a 2s GIF into a 6s clip), container format (MP4 or WebM), and resolution scaling.

3

Convert & Download Video

Click Convert to render the video stream directly in browser memory. Preview the video with full player controls and download instantly.
