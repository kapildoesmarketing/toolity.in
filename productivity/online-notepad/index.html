Frequently Asked Questions

Online Notepad FAQ

Learn more about how Online Notepad protects your privacy, manages local storage, and formats notes.

Are my notes stored on any cloud server or database?
No, never. Toolity's Online Notepad operates 100% on the client side inside your web browser. Your text is saved exclusively in your personal browser's localStorage. No text, notes, or keystrokes are transmitted to any backend servers or external third parties.
Will my notes stay intact if I refresh or close my browser tab?
Yes. Every character you type is automatically written to your browser's persistent local storage. When you return to this page, your text will automatically reload exactly as you left it, even across device reboots (unless you manually clear browser site data or click Clear).
Can I use this Online Notepad offline without an internet connection?
Yes. Because all editing, word counting, text transformations, and downloads execute purely in local JavaScript, you can open the page and write notes completely offline without an active network connection.
What happens if I accidentally click the Clear button?
We've built in an instant Undo protection mechanism. When you click Clear, your previous note is safely held in memory and an interactive toast banner appears with an Undo button, allowing you to restore your note immediately with a single click.
Is there a character or document length limit?
Browser local storage provides approximately 5MB to 10MB of storage per domain, which is equivalent to several million characters or hundreds of pages of text—more than enough for everyday drafting, notes, and documentation.
