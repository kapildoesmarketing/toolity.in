How It Works

How to Split a GIF in 3 Steps

1

Drop a GIF

Drag an animated GIF onto the left pane. The frame slider lets you pick an exact cut point when you need one.

2

Choose how to split

In Settings split into N equal parts, into chunks of N frames, or in two at the selected frame.

3

Split and download

Click Split GIF. Each part previews on the right — click one to save it alone, or press Download for a ZIP of all parts.

FAQ

GIF Splitter — Frequently Asked Questions

Is my GIF uploaded anywhere?

No. The GIF is decoded frame by frame in your browser and re-encoded on your device with a small open-source encoder. Nothing leaves your computer.

Does it keep the original frame timing?

Yes. Each frame's own delay is preserved, so GIFs with mixed timing (a long pause on the last frame, for example) play exactly as before.

How are parts named?

As name-part01.gif, name-part02.gif… so they sort in order.

Do the parts loop?

Yes, each part loops forever like a normal GIF. Use the GIF Looper on a part if you want it to play once.

Is there a size limit?

Files up to 50 MB are accepted. Decoding keeps every frame in memory, so very long, high-resolution GIFs (thousands of frames) may be slow on phones — shorten them with the GIF Trimmer first.
