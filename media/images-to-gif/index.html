    How It Works

    How to Make a GIF from Images in 3 Steps

    1

    Add images

    Drop two or more JPG, PNG or WebP files onto the left pane (you can add more later). They appear in a list — use the arrows to reorder and × to remove.

    2

    Set the pace

    In Settings choose how long each frame shows (100 ms for animation, 1–2 s for a slideshow), how images of different sizes are fitted, and whether the GIF loops.

    3

    Make and download

    Click Make GIF. Encoding takes a second or two; preview the result on the right and download it.

    FAQ

    Images to GIF Maker — Frequently Asked Questions

    Is my GIF uploaded anywhere?

    No. The GIF is decoded frame by frame in your browser and re-encoded on your device with a small open-source encoder. Nothing leaves your computer.

    Can I mix portrait and landscape images?

    Yes. The output takes the first (or largest) image's size; the others are letterboxed on a transparent background or cropped to fill, whichever you choose in Settings.

    How many images can I use?

    There is no fixed cap, but GIF files grow quickly — 30 full-size photos make a large file. Use the Max width setting to keep it shareable.

    Why are the colours slightly different?

    GIF only allows 256 colours per frame. A palette is built from your images, which is fine for graphics and most photos; gradients and skies may show slight banding.

    Can I use one delay per image?

    Not yet — one delay applies to every frame. Open the result in the GIF Speed Changer if you want to adjust overall speed afterwards.
