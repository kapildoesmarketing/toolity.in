FAQ

Image to Base64 — Frequently Asked Questions

Is my image uploaded anywhere?

No. The image is decoded and processed on a canvas in your browser tab. Nothing is sent to a server, and nothing is kept after you close the page.

Is the original file encoded byte-for-byte?

Yes. The file's exact bytes are encoded — no re-compression — so decoding the string gives back the identical file.

Why is the Base64 bigger than the file?

Base64 stores 3 bytes in 4 text characters, so it is always about 33% larger. Inline only small images (icons, logos under ~10 KB); link larger ones.

Can I decode Base64 back to an image?

Yes — paste a data URI or raw Base64 into the box under the dropzone. Raw strings are recognised by their PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP or SVG signature.

Does it work with SVG?

Yes. SVG is encoded like any other file and the data URI uses the image/svg+xml type.
