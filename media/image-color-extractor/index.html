How It Works

How to Extract Colors from an Image in 3 Steps

1

Upload or Paste an Image

Drop a photo, logo or screenshot onto the card, click to browse, or paste from your clipboard. The palette is extracted instantly on your device.

2

Pick the Palette Size

Choose 4, 6, 8 or 12 colors. Each swatch shows its HEX value and how much of the image it covers. Click the preview to eyedrop any single pixel.

3

Copy or Download

Click a swatch to copy one color, Copy for the whole list, or Download as CSS variables, JSON or a PNG swatch strip. Esc resets.

FAQ

Image Color Extractor — Frequently Asked Questions

Is my image uploaded anywhere?

No. The image is drawn onto a canvas inside your browser and the colors are computed there with JavaScript. Nothing is sent to a server and nothing is stored.

Which image formats are supported?

Any format your browser can display: PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF (first frame), BMP, AVIF and SVG. There is no file-size limit, but very large images are downscaled internally before analysis.

How do I get the exact color of one spot in the image?

Click (or tap) anywhere on the preview. The eyedropper reads that pixel and shows its HEX, RGB and HSL values; click the swatch to copy it in your preferred format.

Why does the palette not include white or black from my image?

By default near-white and near-black pixels are ignored so backgrounds and shadows do not crowd out the real colors. Turn off "Ignore near-white and near-black" in Options to include them.

How are the dominant colors chosen?

The image is downscaled and its pixels are grouped with median-cut quantization into the number of colors you select. Each swatch is the average of its group and the percentage shows how much of the image it covers.
