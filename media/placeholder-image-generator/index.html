FAQ

Placeholder Image Generator — Frequently Asked Questions

What is a placeholder image used for?

Placeholder images stand in for real artwork while a website, app or document is being built. They show the exact dimensions a final image must have, so layouts can be tested before the photography or design work is finished.

Which formats can I download?

PNG (lossless, supports transparency), JPG (smallest for photos, quality adjustable in Options), WebP (modern, small) and SVG (a tiny vector file that stays sharp at any size and can be edited as text).

What is the maximum image size?

Up to 4000 × 4000 pixels per image. Larger values are clamped so the generator stays fast and memory-safe on phones and laptops alike.

Can I copy the image instead of downloading it?

Yes. Click Copy to place the PNG on your clipboard and paste it directly into Figma, Slack, Google Docs, an email or any image editor.

Is anything uploaded to a server?

No. The image is drawn on an HTML canvas inside your browser and exported from there. Nothing leaves your device and nothing is stored.
