FAQ

Timer Widget — Frequently Asked Questions

How do I embed this widget in Notion?

Click Copy in the toolbar to copy the embed URL, open your Notion page, type /embed, paste the link and press Enter. Drag the block handles to fit your column.

Does the timer keep running if I close the page?

Yes. Only the start time is saved in your own browser (local storage), so when you reopen the page the stopwatch or Pomodoro carries on with the right time. Nothing is sent anywhere.

How does the Pomodoro work?

Focus for the set length, then take a short break; after the chosen number of rounds you get a long break. The timer pauses at each switch and can play a chime, so it never runs on through a break you missed.

Why is there no sound?

Browsers only allow sound after you interact with the page, so the chime works once you have pressed Start. Check that Chime is on and the tab is not muted.

Can it count down to a date?

Use the Countdown Timer widget for that. "Days since" here counts up from a past date, like days since launch or since you quit smoking.

Does the embedded widget track visitors?

No. The /embed/ page loads no analytics or third-party scripts and runs entirely in the visitor's browser.
