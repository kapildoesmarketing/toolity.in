FAQ

Flip Clock Widget — Frequently Asked Questions

How do I embed this Flip Clock in Notion?

Click Copy in the toolbar to copy the embed URL, open your Notion page, type /embed, paste the link, and press Enter. Resize the block handles to fit your column.

Where are my custom settings saved?

Every setting is encoded directly in the embed URL query parameters. No account or database is needed, and the link works permanently on any device.

Does the embedded widget include tracking or analytics?

No. The /embed/ page loads zero analytics or third-party tracking scripts and runs 100% locally in the visitor's browser.

Can I edit my widget later after embedding it?

Yes. Click the subtle Customise link in the corner of your embedded clock, or paste your existing embed URL into the Paste existing widget URL field on this page to load all your settings back.
