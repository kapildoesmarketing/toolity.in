How It Works

How to Make a Photo Strip in 3 Steps

1

Pick a layout

Choose a classic 2×6 strip (2, 3 or 4 photos) or a 4×6 grid. Use your camera, or switch to Upload to use photos you already have.

2

Shoot on the countdown

Press the shutter once — the booth counts down, flashes and moves to the next frame by itself. Pick a filter before you start; tap any shot to retake it.

3

Decorate and download

Set the frame colour, drop in a sticker pack, add your logo and a caption or today's date, then download a 300 dpi PNG or JPG ready to print.

FAQ

Photo Booth — Frequently Asked Questions

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. The camera feed, every shot and the finished strip exist only in your browser's memory. The page makes no network requests with your images; closing the tab discards everything.

What size are the downloaded strips?

Strips export at 300 dpi print size: 2×6 inch layouts are 600×1800 px and 4×6 inch layouts are 1200×1800 px, as PNG (lossless) or JPG (smaller).

Can I use it without a webcam?

Yes. Switch the source to Upload on the layout step and pick photos from your device; they are cropped to the layout and go through the same customisation and download steps.

Why does the browser ask for camera permission every time?

Browsers only remember camera permission on secure (https) sites and when you choose Allow rather than Allow once. On iPhone, Safari asks on every visit by design; the booth also stops the camera as soon as you leave the shooting step.

How do I move or remove a sticker?

Drag any sticker on the preview. Tap a sticker to select it, then use the size slider or Remove; Clear all removes the whole pack. Tapping a pack again re-scatters it in new positions.
