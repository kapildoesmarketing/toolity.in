About Toolity

One line: Toolity is a free collection of fast, privacy-first browser tools — everything runs on your device and nothing is uploaded.

Short description: Toolity.in offers 125+ free online tools to compress and convert images, video, GIFs and audio, edit PDFs, generate QR codes and links, format developer data, play classic games and embed widgets in Notion and websites. Every tool runs locally in the browser using modern web platform APIs, so files never leave the user's device. No sign-up, no watermarks.
