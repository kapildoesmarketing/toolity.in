Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026. Transparency on our strict client-side privacy architecture.

1. Core Principle: No Server-Side Data Storage

At Toolity.in, we believe privacy is a fundamental standard. Unlike traditional web services that process calculations and file conversions on remote backends, our tools are constructed to execute 100% locally in your web browser.

When you enter text, upload images, generate QR codes, format code, or perform mathematical calculations, that information resides solely in your computer’s temporary memory (RAM) and is never transmitted to our servers or saved in any database.

2. Local Storage Usage

Toolity.in does not use tracking cookies. We utilize the browser’s standard localStorage API solely to store user interface preferences:

  • Theme Preference: We store whether you prefer Dark Mode or Light Mode (toolity_theme) so your preference persists across sessions.

This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time through your browser settings.

3. Third-Party CDNs & Infrastructure

To provide fast static delivery, Toolity relies on trusted Content Delivery Networks (CDNs) for static web assets:

  • Google Fonts: For serving typography (Inter font).
  • Iconify: For loading lightweight open-source Lucide vector icons.
  • GitHub Pages: For global static hosting under SSL encryption.

4. Advertising & Monetization

To keep all tools free and accessible without subscriptions:

  • Homepage is Ad-Free: Our homepage and navigation directories never host disruptive ads.
  • Tool Pages: Select individual tool pages may feature non-intrusive banner advertisements provided by vetted ad networks (such as Adsterra or Google AdSense).
  • These ad networks may use anonymous cookies to serve relevant advertisements according to standard industry privacy practices. You can opt out of personalized ads via your browser settings or privacy extensions.
